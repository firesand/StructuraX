# Design reconstruction wave 3: bounded EC3 local-3 shear

The EC3 steel path now checks local-3 shear for solid rectangular sections and applies the manual's high-shear reduction to pure local-2 bending at a station. The supported demand is `V3` with optional `M2`; nonzero `P`, `V2`, `M3`, or torsion at that station is rejected. Other section types with nonzero `V3` are rejected. This keeps the implemented check within one independently verifiable EC3 branch rather than presenting an incomplete general biaxial interaction as a pass. Concrete local-3 shear remains rejected by the shared demand validation.

The supported section's shear area is `Av=A`, so `Vpl,Rd = A fy/(sqrt(3) gamma_M0)` under EC3 6.2.6(2)-(3). The existing EC3 path uses `gamma_M0=1.00`; this slice adds no National Annex configuration. When `|V3|/Vpl,Rd > 0.5`, the reduction is `rho=(2|V3|/Vpl,Rd-1)^2` and `Mv,Rd=(1-rho)Mc,Rd` under EC3 6.2.8(2)-(3), as transcribed in the supplied CSI *Steel Frame Design Manual Eurocode 3-2005*, printed pages 6-2–6-3. The manual explicitly assigns this general reduced-capacity expression to “all other sections,” including solid rectangles. The station ratio is the maximum of local-3 shear utilization and `|M2|/Mv,Rd`. In an EC3 result governed by this branch, `phi_mn22` reports the effective reduced resistance at the governing station, so `mr_minor_ratio = |M2|/phi_mn22`; otherwise it retains the section resistance. AISC results retain their existing unmodified capacity semantics.

Evidence is in `crates/sap-design/src/shear.rs` and the EC3 design tests in `crates/sap-design/src/lib.rs`. For an 8 by 4 in rectangle with `fy=50 ksi`, a hand substitution gives `A=32 in²`, `Vpl,Rd=32*50/sqrt(3)=923.7604 kip`, and `Mpl,Rd=32*50=1600 kip-in`. At `V3=0.75Vpl,Rd`, `rho=0.25` and `Mv,Rd=1200 kip-in`; a demand `M2=0.875Mpl,Rd=1400 kip-in` therefore produces a bending ratio `1.1667` and an overstress result. Unit tests also reject nonrectangular sections and unsupported simultaneous force components. A concrete-design regression confirms nonzero `V3` is rejected rather than ignored.

This is not full EC3 member design or CSI parity. Major-axis shear interaction, axial force, biaxial bending, combined local-2/local-3 shear, torsion, shear buckling, and nonrectangular local-3 shear are outside this checked branch and cannot be inferred as passing from these results. The broader preexisting EC3 interaction/capacity path retains its existing limitations.
