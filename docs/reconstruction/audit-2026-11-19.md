# Repair round after the outside audit of 2026-11-19

Date: 2026-11-19. Scope note for one bounded set of repairs. It does not
change the project status: vendor-runtime acceptance and broad parity remain
unestablished, and no percentage of compatibility is claimed.

An outside audit of the whole program (design, sections, solver, file
formats, API and windows) listed about sixty defects. This note records which
of them were found to be real, what was repaired, what is now refused instead
of computed, and what stays open. The identifiers D.. and G.. are those of
[tutorial-video-progress.md](tutorial-video-progress.md).

No number of the audit is a reference. Every repair has a test whose expected
value is a hand calculation, a closed form or a number the vendor publishes
with its example, and each new test was shown to fail without its change.

## How the claims were checked

Six verifiers took the audit's findings by area and tried to refute each one
against the code of this branch and the vendor material: the lines, the
entry point that reaches them, the page of the vendor manual, and whether
the ledger already had it. Formulas were read from the page image, not from
extracted text; one of the defects below (D85) had come from a radical lost
in text extraction. The result:

- about half of the claims are wrong answers from input the program accepts;
- about a dozen are refuted (list at the end);
- the rest are minor, already in the ledger, deliberate, or features that
  were never built.

A planner then worked each confirmed item out by hand from the vendor pages
and decided, item by item, between building the vendor's rule and refusing
the input by name where the vendor material has no rule. Four implementers
repaired one area each; a reviewer read every source change, merged the four patches
and ran the checks on the merged tree.

## What changes for a model that ran before

Results that move:

- **Steel, AISC.** A pipe under bending and torsion (D85, higher ratio). The
  compression strength of slender boxes, angles, double angles, tees and
  channels (D86, lower), and of I sections with a slender web (D86, slightly
  higher: 506.1 kip on the vendor's Example 002, where 498.1 was reported).
  Double angles and double channels everywhere `t2` is used (D87, D97): `t2`
  is the total width, as the vendor defines it. A passing I section with a
  slender flange now reads "OK" (D91).
- **Steel, EC3.** Class 3 sections take the elastic modulus (D90, higher
  ratio).
- **Concrete.** Column shear with axial force (D94) and at 45° under EC2
  (D95); the minor-axis magnifier of rectangular columns (D96); the
  compression steel of doubly reinforced beams is reported (D99); EC2 beam
  shear with the optimised strut angle, the size factor and the stirrup
  yield in the minimum (D100, D101); the ACI minimum eccentricity (D102).
- **Solver.** V13 and V23 of thin shells and plates (D105, larger by the
  factor that was missing); reactions of supports reached through a
  constraint (D106); response-spectrum cases with more than one direction
  (D107); a history that continues from a nonlinear static case with area
  loads (D108); cables defined by a horizontal tension in cases with other
  loads (D109); models in N and m or N and mm with a fixed link (D110).
- **Import.** Rebar materials and the rebar of concrete sections from native
  files (D114); sections of the tags "2L", "2C", "CR" and "WW" from the older
  library format (D98); a vendor S2K file with area stiffness modifiers
  (D119).
- **Tables of the API.** Numbers read back exactly (D115); the section,
  material, case, pattern and combination tables no longer invent or
  overwrite values (D116 to D118).

Input that ran before and is now refused by name:

- EC3: a member with compression and a moment (D89); a Class 4 section, and a
  tee whose stem is beyond 9 ε in bending (D90). **This stops EC3 design at
  nearly every column of a 3D frame** until the interaction factors exist
  (G71). The earlier result for such members was a plain sum that can be
  unconservative, so it was not kept.
- An I section with unequal flanges, under AISC and under EC3 with a major
  moment (D88); a pipe more slender than 0.45 E/Fy (D86).
- A response-spectrum case with two functions in one direction (D107), with
  directions combined other than by SRSS (D122), with a modal combination
  other than CQC, SRSS or Absolute, or with a load at an angle (D141; vendor
  Example 1-024 has such a case, "RS-10PCT", which was run as CQC).
- Members with tension or compression limits under axial span loads of both
  signs (D111).
- A native file whose diaphragm constraint is split per level (D113; no
  corpus file has it).
- Native export of a rebar material whose two yield values differ (D114).
- A new section staged in the tables without all its columns (D116).

One refused member stops the whole steel design run and leaves no steel
results; this was so before, but there are more refusals now. A single I
section with unequal flanges or a too slender pipe therefore stops the design
of an AISC model as well (D127).

## What was not built

The refusals above stand for features that are now ledger rows: G70 (AISC F4
and F5), G71 (EC3 interaction factors), G72 (Class 4), G73 (EC3 torsion), G74
and G80 (other response-spectrum combinations), G75, G76, G77.

G71 is the one that matters most. The v19 manual names Annex B (Method 2) of
EN 1993-1-1 as the vendor's default and says the method is "not repeated in
this manual"; the v24 manual is the same. The project rule keeps a rule that
is not in the vendor material out, so nothing was built from the standard
itself. Whether the standard may serve as the source is the user's decision.

D112 is partial: a constrained degree of freedom that is very soft no longer
makes a nonlinear case "singular", but the dynamic case then needs a looser
tolerance than the default. The cause is the penalty form of the constraints
(D03b).

## Defects found during the round and left open

Found by the planner and the implementers, not by the audit.
The first three give unconservative results today:

- **D128** AISC flexural-torsional buckling of tees, angles, double angles
  and channels is missing. D86 repaired the factor Q of these shapes only;
  their compression strength is still not the vendor's.
- **D129** Noncompact and slender flexure of boxes, pipes, channels, tees and
  angles is taken as the plastic moment; a noncompact one reads "OK".
- **D130** Concrete torsion is neither designed nor refused.
- D126 (EC3 Class 3 under high shear, as the manual prints it), D127 (Auto
  Select stops on a refused candidate), D131 (EC2 α_cc), D132 (column
  magnifier details, σ_cp).

Recorded from the audit without a repair: D133 to D140, G78, G79, and an
addition to D25. D138 (what the native writer still writes differently from
vendor files, and the rebar record it writes since D114) can only be settled
by opening such a file in the vendor program.

## Claims that were refuted

- Hinges "ignored" in a nonlinear history case: that case type is the
  vendor's nonlinear modal history, in which hinges do not act (Analysis
  Reference p. 148). What differs is the method of integration (D136).
- Nodal stresses of combinations taken from averaged values: the code
  derives them again from the combined forces.
- EC3 shear checked without separating V2 and V3: the vendor reports them
  separately, as the code does.
- "Two steel design paths" in the windows; the shear hinge under P-Delta
  losing a couple; the solid material angle having the wrong sign (it follows
  the manual; the joint angle is the open question, D135); the value of `m_GuideDir`
  in the native writer.
- The audit's worked numbers for EC3 beam-columns could not be reproduced;
  the defect itself is real (D89), with other numbers.

## Not verified

- Nothing was run in the vendor program. Agreement with SAP2000 rests on its
  manuals and published examples only.
- The windows were not opened; their repairs are tested through the code of
  the windows without a display.
- The two lines of the command line that leave cables out of design have no
  test. EC2 doubly reinforced beams above fck 50 MPa keep the earlier form
  and have no test.
- D105: the two strip tests bend one way with ν = 0 and one element across
  the width; no plate that bends two ways was checked. Example 2-008 on the
  20 x 20 mesh is an ignored (slow) test and was not run.
- No test covers a model in which the new link penalty meets buckling.
